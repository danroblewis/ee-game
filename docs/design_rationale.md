All three concepts converge on the same core (semantic zoom over one authoritative sim, Falstad-feel living schematic, one-click probes with auto-everything scopes, degrade-never-destroy, contracts verified by real measurement, ungated primitives, blueprint libraries, Zachtronics histograms) — so the judgment is about skeletons and unique grafts.

**Skeleton: Concept 1 (Common Ground).** It has the cleanest answer to the hardest open question — the world/schematic relationship. "The world IS the canvas; the real-world view is a semantic-zoom re-skin of the same plane" makes the signature dive-from-village-lights-to-transistor a single uninterrupted gesture with one renderer and no modal seams. It also has the deepest editor/UX design (Figma-style edit-in-context subcircuits as both abstraction ladder and progression) and the most complete damage/repair model.

**Grafted from Concept 2 (OHMSTEAD):** geography as an electrical quantity — wire runs stamped as per-meter R plus lumped L/C, so distance genuinely causes droop and low-pass; the neutral-ground tap/cut metagame; TDR-based tap detection ("every attack has a physics signature"); instruments-as-buildable-devices; the executable-contract verifier detail; rival-schematic fog ("you learn about enemies by measuring").

**Grafted from Concept 3 (LIVEWIRE):** the strongest anti-grief structure — the service entrance with an unbypassable auto-resetting main fuse and islanding guarantee; the Party Line as named diplomacy/hacking surface; thermal grid segments; watts-as-currency contract economy; the mode lineup (Sandbox / Co-op / Free Market / Blackout); the mechanic→sim grounding table discipline; the sharpest MVP framing ("two friends brown each other out in the browser").

**Contradictions resolved:** geometry is electrical only at world scale (runs), lumped inside boards; rival visibility is a room-template rule (open in co-op, world-view-only in PvP); economy buys capacity/ratings/quantity, never access to primitives; persistent rooms whose sim pauses when empty (no offline grinding).
